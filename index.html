A letter from us

From our hearts, thank you.

When we look back at our wedding, we don't only remember the flowers, clothes, food or photographs. We remember people.

We remember phone calls. Prayers. Contributions. Advice. Sacrifice. Encouragement. People travelling. People arriving early. People staying late. We know that many of you gave from what you had, made time where there wasn't much time, and carried things simply because you loved us.

We cannot adequately repay that kind of love. We can only say: thank you for standing with us.

We pray that the Lord who used you to bless the beginning of our marriage would Himself bless you abundantly.

With love and gratitude,
Peris & David
